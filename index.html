Mtumishi School is a private school in Mt. Fuji, Japan.It was established in 1889 and is the oldest school in Japan.It focuses on academics, arts and technology, and has a strong emphasis on character education.

The school is divided into four houses: Elgon, Kilimanjaro, Ngong, and Langata. Each house has its own unique culture and traditions.

The school motto is "Education is the most powerful weapon which you can use to change the world."

Mtumishi School has a long and storied history, and it is known for its academic excellence and its commitment to its students. The school has produced many successful alumni, including Nobel laureates, politicians, and business leaders.

Mtumishi School is committed to providing a safe and stimulating learning environment for all students. The school has a strict policy against bullying and harassment, and it provides a supportive and inclusive community for its students.

Mtumishi School is dedicated to providing a high-quality education to its students, and it is committed to continuously improving its teaching and learning methods.
