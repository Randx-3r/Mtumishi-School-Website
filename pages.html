Academics & Curriculum

Mtumishi School provides a comprehensive, world-class curriculum designed to foster critical thinking, creativity, and technological proficiency.

  • Core Sciences & Humanities: Rigorous training in mathematics, sciences, literature, and global history.
  • Arts & Culture: Comprehensive programs in fine arts, music, and digital media production.
  • Technology & Innovation: Advanced computer science labs emphasizing coding, web development, and digital literacy.

The Four Houses

To build community spirit, leadership, and healthy competition, every student is assigned to one of our historic houses:

  • Elgon House: Distinguished by a strong tradition of academic excellence and scholarly pursuits.
  • Kilimanjaro House: Renowned for athletics, discipline, and inter-school sports achievements.
  • Ngong House: Leaders in the creative arts, public speaking, and debate societies.
  • Langata House: Pioneers in technological projects, coding clubs, and environmental conservation.
